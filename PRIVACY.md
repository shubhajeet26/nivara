# NIVARA — Privacy & Data Sovereignty Policy

## 1. Privacy-by-Design in Crises

In emergency scenarios, individuals should never be forced to sacrifice their civil liberties or location privacy in order to find safety. Nivara follows a strict **zero-surveillance, privacy-first architectural standard**.

---

## 2. Real-Time Location Handling

- **On-Device Positioning**: Geolocation coordinates retrieved via the browser's Geolocation API or device GNSS chip are processed strictly on the client.
- **No Background Beaconing**: Nivara does NOT upload user coordinates to remote servers while you browse the map, view shelters, or inspect evacuation routes.
- **Explicit Fallback Mode**: If you deny browser location permissions, Nivara immediately falls back to a neutral district landmark without disabling the user interface or nagging with repetitive popups.
- **No Location History**: The application does not maintain a breadcrumb log of your past positions.

---

## 3. Community Hazard Reports

- **User-Initiated Only**: A report is only transmitted or saved when you explicitly press the "Publish Field Report" button.
- **Location Tagging Disclosure**: Coordinates attached to a report represent the hazard location, clearly displayed to the user prior to submission.
- **Pseudonymous Attribution**: Field reports in Phase 1 use local session designations and do not require email addresses, phone numbers, or social media logins.

---

## 4. Trackers and Telemetry

- **Zero Commercial Analytics**: Nivara contains no third-party marketing SDKs, trackers, cookies, or behavioral advertising pixels.
- **Data Retention**: Phase 1 session data is stored locally in client memory and can be cleared at any time by refreshing or resetting browser storage.
